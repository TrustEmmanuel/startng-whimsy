For builders

Got a game? Put it on start.ng

Any static browser game works: HTML, JavaScript, three.js, Phaser. Players bring their start.ng character, scores and friends.

built by the HNG community
1

Build it

Make a static browser game. Keep it playable on a laptop, a phone, and the bus.

2

Push it to GitHub

A public repo with a ready-to-play index.html at the top or in dist/, plus a cover.png.

3

Publish it

One API call with your repo link, no key needed. Or hand your AI start.ng/docs and let it do the lot.

4

Approve it once

Open the link you get back and it's live at yourgame.start.ng, with its own leaderboard if you want one.

About

Nigeria's home for browser games.

start.ng is built by the HNG community. Every game is free to play in your browser with no download, and many are made in Naija: danfo rush hour, jollof catching, Lagos markets and more.

We're also building a home for web games from across Africa. If you make games anywhere on the continent, you can put yours on start.ng for free.

More about start.ng →

no downloads · no wahala
What is start.ng?

Nigeria's home for free browser games, built by the HNG community. Pick a game and it plays right in your browser.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Open a game and play. An account only comes in when you want a start.ng character, scores and friends.

What's the daily tournament?

Every day, a few community games are picked for 24 hours. Play them, post a score, climb the leaderboard.

Are the games really Naija?

Plenty of them. Danfo traffic, jollof, Lagos markets, owambe, ayo, whot — plus arcade games from builders across Africa.

Is it free to publish my game?

Yes. Free to play, free to publish. Push your repo, make one API call, approve the link once — then every push updates it.
